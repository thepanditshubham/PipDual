## What you can do without asking

- Fork the code, modify it, run it and redistribute it under the AGPL.
- Say that your project is "based on Pip Duel" or "a fork of Pip Duel", as long
  as the statement is true and not presented as endorsement.
- Link to the upstream repository and discuss the project.

## What you must not do without written permission

- Use the name "Pip Duel" (or a confusingly similar name) for a fork, product,
  service, domain, app listing, or social account.
- Use the logo, wordmark or tagline in a way that suggests the project owner
  endorses, sponsors or maintains your fork.
- Register a domain, trademark or package name that is confusingly similar to
  "Pip Duel".

## Forks

Forks must be renamed. A clear statement such as *"This is an unofficial fork of
Pip Duel. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Pip Duel maintainers."* is
welcome, provided the new name and branding are distinct.
